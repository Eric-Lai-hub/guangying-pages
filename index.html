隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 12 日 / Last updated: 12 September 2026

中文

光影调色不收集任何个人信息。

  • 照片、视频、实况照片与参考图全部在你的 iPhone 上处理，App 不发起任何网络请求，也不上传任何内容。
  • App 只在你主动选择时读取相册里的照片与视频，读取的内容仅用于在设备上生成预览与导出结果。
  • 导出的图片、视频与 .cube 文件保存到你指定的位置（相册或分享目标）。不会修改你的原始照片。
  • 相机权限只在你在应用内主动拍摄时使用，拍摄结果同样只留在本机。
  • 不含统计、广告或任何第三方 SDK，因此没有数据可被收集、共享或出售。
  • 不要求注册或登录，不索取任何账号信息；风格库与备份文件都保存在你自己的设备上。
  • 你不必提供任何个人信息即可使用本 App；如通过邮件联系我们，我们只会看到你发来的邮箱地址与内容。

English

Color Grade Studio does not collect any personal information.

  • Photos, videos, Live Photos and reference images are processed entirely on your iPhone. The app makes no network requests and uploads nothing.
  • The app reads photos and videos from your library only when you pick them, and only to render previews and exports on the device.
  • Exports — images, videos and .cube files — are written to the destination you choose (Photos or a share target). Original photos are never modified.
  • Camera access is used only when you choose to shoot inside the app, and the result also stays on your device.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs — there is no data to collect, share or sell.
  • No account is required and no personal details are asked for; your style library and backups live on your own device.
  • You can use the app without providing any personal information. If you email us, we only see the address and the message you send.

权限说明 · Permissions

  • 照片 · Photos：导入要调色的照片与视频，并把导出结果保存回相册。
    To import the photos and videos you want to grade, and to save your exports back to your library.
  • 相机 · Camera：应用内直接拍摄并调色。
    To shoot and grade a photo without leaving the app.
